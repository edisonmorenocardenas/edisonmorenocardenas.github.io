Research interests

Wireless communications and next-generation mobile networks (5G/6G), network economics and resource management, Internet of Things (IoT), artificial intelligence and machine learning, autonomous and intelligent systems, control and automation systems, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), computer vision and intelligent sensing, signal processing, game theory, and technology-driven business models.

Research projects

  1. National University of San Antonio Abad of Cusco, Peru
    12/2025 - present: Design and simulation of a PI controller applied to a permanent magnet synchronous motor.
    This project aims to design and simulate a PI controller for a permanent magnet synchronous motor, improving stability and performance in speed or position control. The system will be modelled and evaluated through simulation in order to validate an efficient, low-cost control strategy for industrial and electric mobility applications.

  2. Universidad Politécnica Salesiana, Ecuador
    12/2024 - present: Analysis of simulated environments for clustering and microservices management for Edge and Fog Computing in IoT networks.
    This project focuses on the analysis of clustering strategies and the management of microservices in Internet of Things (IoT) networks based on edge computing and fog computing architectures. The research addresses the optimization of computational resource allocation, task distribution and data processing with the aim of reducing latency, improving network performance and enhancing scalability. The project evaluates distributed computing environments through simulations and experimental validation to identify efficient resource management strategies for IoT applications.

  3. Universitat Politècnica de València, Spain
    09/2022 - 07/2024: Radio access network evolution towards 6G for massive and low latency services.
    This project has focused on addressing the limitations of current 5G networks to support future Internet of Everything (IoE) applications. The aim is to define and develop new generations of wireless networks (B5G and 6G) capable of seamlessly integrating IoE functionalities.

    09/2019 - 09/2022: Dynamic Network Slicing in 5G Radio Access (ATLAS).
    This project has addressed the management of physical and virtual resource allocation by the infrastructure provider to tenants, as well as the business models associated with this provider-tenant relationship. This project has been funded by the European Union (EU) and the Spanish Ministry of Science and Innovation.
